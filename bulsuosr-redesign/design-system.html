One token set, one component set, one page template

Everything on the Office of the Student Regent site reads from the rules below. Colour, type, spacing and components are declared once as semantic tokens; no component hard-codes a value, and every measurement on this page is read live from the stylesheet in your browser — so this page cannot drift from the site.

Colour

One institutional red carries the BulSU identity; everything else is paper, ink and a status set. The palette is deliberately narrow — a student government site earns trust by looking like a record, not a product launch.

Type

Fraunces for institutional display, IBM Plex Sans for reading, IBM Plex Mono for codes, dates and labels. Body copy is 16px at 1.65 line-height; nothing that a student must read is set below 14px.

Space & radius

Spacing is a 4px scale; radii are small by design — square-ish corners read as documents, pills read as chips. Only the tab icon and avatars are round.

Rules the build enforces

These are checked by the repository's own suite (71 assertions across 14 test files) and by a jsdom smoke pass that walks every route.

  • Body copy ≥ 16px at 1.65 line-height; no reading text below 14px.
  • Interactive targets at least 44×44px on touch, including icon-only buttons.
  • Every dialog traps Tab, closes on Escape and returns focus to what opened it.
  • No information is carried by colour alone — status always has a word beside it.
  • Icons are inline SVG from one stroke set; decorative icons are hidden from assistive tech, meaningful ones are labelled.
  • No horizontal scroll from 320px up; long titles wrap instead of pushing layout.
